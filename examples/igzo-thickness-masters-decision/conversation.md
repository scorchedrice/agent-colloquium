# IGZO Thickness Master's-Direction Deliberation Record

## 1. Initial user question

In an undergraduate IGZO experiment, oxygen was known to influence mobility. The user observed that mobility appeared to degrade both above and below an optimal channel thickness. The user asked whether reducing the optimal thickness is a sound master's direction, rather than simply continuing the earlier experiment.

## 2. Assistant request for missing practical constraints

Before evaluating the direction, the assistant asked only for the missing practical constraints: the intended IGZO deposition method and the measurement and characterization access that would be available. No experiment, literature search, fabrication, equipment operation, or external contact was authorized.

The user specified ALD IGZO deposition, Hall measurement access, and AFM/TEM via collaborators. This supplied context is unverified: it records available access and a recalled observation, not a verified device history, measured mechanism, or completed characterization result.

## 5. Independent role outcomes

Four fresh independent role outputs were prepared from the same supplied context. Each role was isolated from the other first-pass outputs.

### Fresh independent Domain Analyst output

The thickness trend could reflect more than one mechanism, including oxygen-related carrier control, interface or contact effects, morphology, composition variation, or measurement artifacts. A sound direction must predict a distinguishing observation for each mechanism before treating a reduced optimum as an objective.

### Fresh independent Evidence Reviewer output

The undergraduate observation and the oxygen explanation are not yet established evidence. Literature verification and a traceable measurement record are needed before claiming that ALD IGZO should reduce the optimum or that a particular mechanism is causal.

### Fresh independent Feasibility Reviewer output

ALD IGZO deposition and Hall measurement access make a bounded study plausible, while AFM/TEM via collaborators can be valuable only if sample requirements, comparable conditions, and turnaround are known. The scope is feasible only as a controlled, repeatable pilot rather than an open-ended optimization campaign.

### Fresh independent Contrarian output

Reducing an apparent optimum may be an attractive process target without being a master's contribution. Unless controls rule out contact, composition, morphology, and measurement explanations, the work risks becoming re-optimization with a lower number rather than mechanism-backed research.
